# Store listing (draft copy)

**Name:** Agentfolio
**Subtitle (iOS, 30):** Your portfolio, explained by AI
**Short description (Play, 80):** See your Angel One portfolio clearly, with AI insights and approval-only trading.

**Description:**

Agentfolio connects to your Angel One account and shows your whole portfolio
at a glance: value, today's move, a P&L heatmap, sector allocation and how you
compare with the NIFTY 50.

Tap any stock for its chart, fundamentals scored in plain language,
technical signals and an AI summary written for beginners. Ask the finance
agent anything about your holdings.

With Premium, a trading agent suggests trades from your own risk profile.
Nothing is ever placed until you approve it, with Face ID or your passcode,
and orders over your limits are blocked before they reach your broker.

- Hide balances with one tap, for using the app in public
- Optional app lock
- Market news with sentiment, sector performance and market breadth
- A daily portfolio brief on WhatsApp
- A what-if rebalancer to test ideas without trading

Agentfolio is not a broker or investment adviser, and nothing in it is a
recommendation. Trading involves risk.

**Category:** Finance. **Content rating:** Everyone / 4+ (no user-generated public content).

**Screenshots to capture** (phone 6.7", tablet 12.9"/10"): Home, a stock's
Overview, Fundamentals score, Agent chat, Trade with a proposal, the approval
sheet, Markets. Use the demo account; never a real portfolio.
